# Delete your Interlude account

You can delete your Interlude account and its data at any time.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open Interlude and go to **Settings**.
2. Scroll down and tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you're signed out.

## Without the app

Email **Zainabid7272@gmail.com** from the email address you sign in with, with the subject **"Delete my Interlude account"**. We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.

## What gets deleted

- Your sign-in, name, email, profile photo and username
- Your friends, friend requests and challenges
- Your rating, answers and match results
- Your notification address and settings
- Any room that never finished

Matches you finished stay in your opponents' history, with you removed from them, so other players don't lose their records. Nothing is kept for later recovery.
