# Interlude Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 November 2026_

Interlude ("the app") is a free, two-player music guessing game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how you can delete it. Questions: **Zainabid7272@gmail.com**.

## What we collect

| Data | Where it comes from | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email address, profile photo | Your Google account, when you sign in with Google | To create your account and show your name and photo to friends and opponents |
| Username | You choose it | So friends can find you |
| Friends and friend requests | Your actions in the app | To show your friends list and let you challenge friends |
| Match data: rooms, answers, response times, scores, results, rating | Playing matches | To run matches, grade answers, and show results, leaderboards and head-to-head records |
| Notification address (an Expo push token) | Your phone, only if you allow notifications | To send challenges and friend requests to your phone |
| Settings (search visibility, notification choices) | You | To respect your choices |

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, files, or advertising ID. There are **no ads** and no tracking or analytics SDKs.

## How it's used and shared

Your data is used only to run Interlude. It is never sold. It is processed by these services, which run the app:

- **Supabase**: stores your account and game data.
- **Google**: sign-in.
- **Expo and Firebase Cloud Messaging**: deliver notifications, only if you turn them on.
- **Spotify, Apple (iTunes) and Deezer**: the app looks up artist and song information and streams 30-second previews from them. These requests don't include your name or email.

Other players can see your display name, username, photo, and your scores and rating on leaderboards. You can hide yourself from player search and the global leaderboard in **Settings → Show me in player search**.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is erased straight away (see below). Matches you finished stay in your opponents' history, with you removed from them.

## Deleting your account

- **In the app:** Settings → **Delete account**.
- **Without the app:** email **Zainabid7272@gmail.com** from the address you sign in with, asking to delete your Interlude account. We'll delete it within 30 days. Details: [delete-account.md](delete-account.md).

## Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules limit each player to their own data and the matches they're in.

## Children

Interlude isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Music

Interlude is an independent fan-made game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by any artist, record label, Spotify, Apple or Deezer. Song information comes from Spotify, and previews are streamed from iTunes and Deezer; all music belongs to its owners. Rights holders can contact **Zainabid7272@gmail.com** and we'll respond promptly.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
